Search for real customer counts (4 October 2026)
WebSearch: "Starbucks number of orders with syrup and nondairy milk data customization statistics".
Results were news articles on the share of drinks that are customized in general (tastingtable.com,
mfour.com) and on the end of the nondairy surcharge (worldcoffeeportal.com, scrippsnews.com). None gave
counts of orders with a syrup, a nondairy milk, a topping or whipped cream, or of the overlaps. The
order counts in Section 7 and Tasks 4 and 5 are therefore made up and labeled as made up.
